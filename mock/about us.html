About Us

Nova Bites was founded by Chiamaka Eze,
a chef who grew up between her grandmother's kitchen in Abakiliki and good trucks in Lagos.
In 2026, she decided to bring both worlds together,
blending timeless Nigerian recipes with the speed and creativtiy of global street food.
Every dish on our menu tells two stories at once: where it's going.
Our mission is simple: bold, honest food, made fast, served with pride
